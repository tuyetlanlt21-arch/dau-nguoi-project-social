## About the Project

**DẤU NGƯỜI** is a student-led social fundraising initiative developed as part of SSG105 at FPT University.

The project was created with a simple belief: meaningful contributions do not always begin with grand gestures. Sometimes, they begin with everyday moments, shared experiences, and the willingness to care.

Through creative fundraising activities, DẤU NGƯỜI transforms ordinary purchases and memorable photographs into opportunities to contribute to a meaningful cause.

The initiative supports the spirit of **Kindness Fieldtrip 9**, a university-organized community program dedicated to expressing gratitude and appreciation to wounded veterans and people who have contributed to the country, particularly those receiving care at the Long Đất Nursing Center for Wounded Veterans and Meritorious People.

## Our Mission

DẤU NGƯỜI aims to connect young people with the values of gratitude, compassion, and social responsibility.

Our objectives are to:

- Raise funds through student-organized commercial activities.
- Encourage meaningful participation in community initiatives.
- Promote appreciation for previous generations and their contributions.
- Demonstrate how creativity and teamwork can create positive social impact.
- Preserve meaningful memories through photography and storytelling.

## Fundraising Activities

The project combines three accessible fundraising experiences.

### Photobooth – Capture a Moment

A photography experience where participants can capture memories with friends while supporting the fundraising initiative.

Each photograph represents more than a personal keepsake. It symbolizes a small contribution toward a shared purpose.

### Food & Beverages – Share a Little Joy

The team organizes the sale of popular snacks and beverages, including:

- Bánh tráng
- Trà tắc

These activities help generate fundraising revenue while creating opportunities for students to participate and connect.

### Community Engagement – Spread the Message

Beyond fundraising, DẤU NGƯỜI encourages students to reflect on gratitude, collective responsibility, and the value of giving back to the community.

## Project Portfolio

This repository contains the official digital portfolio documenting the journey of DẤU NGƯỜI.

The website presents the project's story through an editorial-inspired, single-page experience featuring photography, storytelling, and activity documentation.

It covers the project's background, objectives, planning, fundraising activities, outcomes, community experiences, and reflections.

The portfolio also documents activities associated with Kindness Fieldtrip 9 while distinguishing the team's fundraising work from the university-organized program.

## Getting Started

### 1. Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/tuyetlanlt21-arch/dau-nguoi-project-social.git
```

### 2. Navigate to the Project

```bash
cd dau-nguoi-project-social
```

### 3. Run Locally

Open `index.html` directly in your browser.

Alternatively, run a local static server:

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit:

```text
http://localhost:8000
```

No additional dependencies or build process are required.

## Deployment

The portfolio is deployed on Vercel.

**Production URL:** https://dau-nguoi-project-social.vercel.app/

As a static website, the project can also be hosted on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or other static hosting services.

All referenced assets must be included during deployment.
